A few useful details.

Is Trio just a chat app?

Chat is one way to work with Trio. Its tools can read information and carry out supported actions in connected apps, within the access and workflows configured for the workspace.

Who handles the underlying setup?

Workspaces are configured with you. The direction is a managed customer experience where the private engine, models, and added processing capacity stay behind the scenes. You focus on your apps and the work they support.

What does Trio do with my context?

Trio uses conversation history and relevant app information to help with a task. App permissions, connected services, data handling, and retention should be agreed during setup. External integrations may involve their own providers.

Does every answer get an independent review?

No. Review is a configured workflow, not a guarantee on every answer. Results should be checked against the task and its evidence, and important actions may require an approval before proceeding.

Can I sign up and start immediately?

Access is currently arranged through a conversation and guided setup. Broader browser-based onboarding and expanded capacity are part of the next phase.
